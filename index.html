Read the market correctly

Node price is the RTD locational marginal price. It is not a retail tariff or a final settlement price.

Generation and market requirement refer to the entire Visayas grid, not ILECO I or the selected node.

Scheduled power preserves the source sign, including zero and negative values. These NL resource rows are not confirmed ILECO I demand.

Hourly and daily values are arithmetic means of available observations. They are not energy-weighted settlement prices or MWh totals.
